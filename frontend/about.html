About Attendance Atlas

Smart attendance tracking system for students and teachers

🎯

Our Purpose

Attendance Atlas is designed to replace traditional attendance methods with a modern, automated, and error-free system that saves time and reduces manual errors.

⚙️

Key Features

  • Real-time attendance marking
  • Student-wise and subject-wise reports
  • Smart analytics and insights
  • CSV and PDF export support
  • Role-based access control
  • Secure authentication system
🚀

Why Choose Us?

Our system provides detailed analytics to help institutions make informed decisions. It's user-friendly, scalable, and designed for modern educational needs.

🎓

Academic Project

Developed as part of a college curriculum to demonstrate real-world application of Flask, Python, and modern web development technologies.

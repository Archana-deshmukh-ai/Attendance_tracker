About Attendance Atlas

Smart attendance tracking system for students and teachers

🎯 Purpose

Attendance Atlas is designed to replace traditional attendance methods with a modern, automated, and error-free system.

⚙️ Features

  • Real-time attendance marking
  • Student-wise and subject-wise reports
  • Smart analytics and insights
  • CSV and PDF export support

🚀 Why Choose Us?

Our system saves time, reduces manual errors, and provides detailed analytics to help institutions make informed decisions.
